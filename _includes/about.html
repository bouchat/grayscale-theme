About Braver Measures

Braver Measures Consulting helps value-driven organizations address meaningful problems, create social impact, and drive political change by turning complex research, evidence, and analysis into clear decisions and executable projects. You get high-level independent judgment and thought partnership, not just project execution support.

About Sirus Bouchat

I am a researcher, organizer, and educator with more than a decade of experience in academic and policy research. I have conducted rigorous data-driven analyses and built computational models exploring political phenomena, helped students at all levels of their education shape research agendas and answer burning intellectual questions, and collaborated with leadership in nonprofit and advocacy groups to develop informed strategies for change.

Before starting Braver Measures Consulting, I was a tenure-track professor in political methodology at Northwestern University, and served in research and program management roles at the National Endowment for Democracy, the Carr Center for Human Rights Policy, and the Bündnis für Demokratie und Toleranz-gegen Extremismus und Gewalt. I value justice, critical engagement, and integrating theory with action. You can find out more about my academic credentials and professional experience here.
